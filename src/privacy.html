Privacy

Nihongo Michi — last updated 25 October 2026

Your progress stays with you

Nihongo Michi has no server and no accounts of its own. Your learners, lessons, reviews and settings are saved on your own device: in the Windows app's data folder on a PC, or in the browser's storage on a phone.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose Connect under Settings → Sync, the app asks Google for permission to use a private app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) and to read your email address, which it shows so you know which account is connected. It saves one file per learner there and reads it back on your other devices. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive, and the developer never receives your progress, your email address or your sign-in.

You can stop syncing at any time with Disconnect, remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its hidden data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Nihongo Michi → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Microphone

The record-and-compare exercises use your microphone only while you are recording. The recording stays on your device and is discarded when you leave the exercise.
