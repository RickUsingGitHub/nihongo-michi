Privacy policy

Nihongo Michi — last updated 27 October 2026

Nihongo Michi is a free app for learning Japanese, available as a Windows desktop app and as a web app for phones at rickusinggithub.github.io/nihongo-michi. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and what it is used for. In short: your study progress stays on your own devices and, only if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer does not run a server and never receives any of your information.

1. Information the app stores on your device

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
The name you give each learnerTo tell learners apart and greet you in lessonsOn your device only: the app's data folder on Windows, or the browser's storage on a phone
Study progress: completed lessons, review schedule, XP, streaks, test resultsTo run the course and the spaced-repetition reviews
Settings such as voice, speed and themeTo remember your preferences

The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use cookies. The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any website host, GitHub keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off unless you choose Connect under Settings → Sync or Continue with Google. When you do, you sign in with Google and the app asks for these permissions:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata) Saves one file per learner, containing that learner's name, progress and settings, in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, and reads it back on your other devices. The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
Your email address (openid, email) Shown in the app ("Connected as …") so you know which Google account a learner syncs with. It is not stored anywhere else or used for anything else.

Google sign-in tokens are kept only on your device (encrypted by Windows on a PC). The app talks directly to Google; the information goes from your device to your own Google account and nowhere else.

Nihongo Michi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide sync between your own devices. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, not read by people, and not used to train AI models.

3. Microphone and speech

Speaking practice ("record and compare") uses your microphone only while you are recording. The recording stays on your device and is discarded when you leave the exercise. It is never uploaded.

Japanese audio is spoken by the voices built into your device. Some browsers also offer online voices; if one is selected, the browser sends the lesson text being spoken (never anything about you) to the browser's maker.

4. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Remove a learner from a device: Settings → Remove this learner.
  • Stop syncing: Settings → Sync → Disconnect. This also signs the app out of your Google account on that device.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the synced files from Google Drive: in Google Drive, Settings → Manage apps → Nihongo Michi → Options → Delete hidden app data.

Information is kept until you delete it in one of these ways.

5. Sharing

Nihongo Michi does not share, sell or transfer any information to anyone. The developer has no access to it.
